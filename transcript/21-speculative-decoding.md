## Prose · Where this chapter's claims come from

**The acceptance rule.** With greedy decoding a draft token is kept when it equals the token the target would have picked. When sampling, each draft token is kept with probability min(1, p_target / p_draft), and the first rejected position is resampled from a corrected distribution, so the output follows the target's distribution exactly (Leviathan et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023). Steps 1 and 2 time one request alone and leave out the KV cache read, which is small at this context length (Ch. 4 Step 6).

**EAGLE.** EAGLE drafts with one Transformer layer that reads the target's own hidden states; EAGLE-3 fuses hidden states from a low, a middle and a high layer and trains the head on its own drafts ("training-time test"). SGLang runs it with `--speculative-algorithm EAGLE3`; for Llama models its defaults are 5 draft steps, 4 branches per step and 8 draft tokens verified (SGLang's speculative decoding docs).

**DFlash.** Chen, Liang and Liu, *DFlash: Block Diffusion for Flash Speculative Decoding* (ICML 2026, arXiv 2602.06036). The drafter has 5 layers and drafts a block of 16 positions in one pass; hidden states from 5 target layers are projected into one context feature and added to the keys and values of every draft layer. SGLang runs it with `--speculative-algorithm DFLASH`.

**DSpark.** Cheng, Yu, Shao, Li, Xiong et al. (DeepSeek-AI and Peking University), *DSpark: Confidence-Scheduled Speculative Decoding with Semi-Autoregressive Generation* (arXiv 2607.05147). Its parallel stage is a DFlash backbone; the sequential stage shown here is its Markov head, a low-rank (rank 256) bias from the previous draft token. The confidence head and the hardware-aware prefix scheduler are its Sections 3.2.1 and 3.2.2. DeepSeek reports 60–85% faster per-user generation on DeepSeek-V4-Flash than its previous one-token MTP drafter.

**The comparison.** Step 7's numbers are DSpark's Table 1 for a Qwen3-8B target: all three drafters retrained on the same data, a draft length of 7, chain drafting, temperature 1, and the accepted length counting the target's own token. The DFlash paper's own comparison against EAGLE-3 (tree drafting, temperature 0) reports larger gaps; setups differ, so the numbers are not interchangeable.
