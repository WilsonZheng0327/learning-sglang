## Prose · The batching arithmetic, once

With $B$ sequences decoding together, one step costs $\max(t_{\text{mem}},\; B \cdot t_{\text{tok}})$, so throughput is

$$
\frac{B}{\max(t_{\text{mem}},\; B\,t_{\text{tok}})} \;\text{tokens/s}
$$

linear in $B$ until $B \approx n^* \approx 300$, then flat. Static batching pays for that with idle slots and queueing; continuous batching (Orca, 2022) re-forms the batch at every step, which is why every serving engine does it now.
