## Prose · The prefix arithmetic, once

Position $i$'s key and value are functions of tokens $1..i$, so two requests can share cache exactly for their longest common prefix $m$ and nothing past it. A shared prefix of $s$ tokens across $r$ requests costs $s$ tokens of cache instead of $r \cdot s$, and a newcomer prefills $P - m$ tokens:

$$
\text{TTFT} \approx \max\!\left(t_{\text{mem}},\; 2(P - m)\,t_{\text{tok}}\right)
$$

In a chat of $T$ turns with question $q_t$ and answer $a_t$, prefill without caching sums to $\sum_t (q_t + \sum_{u<t}(q_u + a_u))$ and with it to $\sum_t q_t$. SGLang's `RadixCache` is the tree, reference counts pin the pages of running requests, eviction is least-recently-used over unreferenced nodes, and `schedule_policy=lpm` orders the waiting list by matched length.
