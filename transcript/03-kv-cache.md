## Prose · Cache size, once

Per token, per sequence, the cache holds one $k$ and one $v$ for every head in every layer:

$$
\text{bytes per token} = 2 \cdot L \cdot H_{kv} \cdot d_{head} \cdot \text{bytes per value}
$$

Llama-3-8B: $2 \cdot 32 \cdot 8 \cdot 128 \cdot 2 = 131{,}072$ bytes, so 128 KB per token, or about 1 GB for an 8k-token context. Grouped-query attention is why $H_{kv} = 8$ rather than the 32 query heads.
