## Prose · The memory arithmetic, once

Per token, the cache costs $2 \cdot L \cdot H_{kv} \cdot d_{head} \cdot \text{bytes}$, 128 KB for Llama-3-8B in bf16. The batch cap is

$$
B_{\max} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{HBM} - \text{weights}}{\text{context} \times 128\,\text{KB}} \right\rfloor
$$

Reserving the maximum length per request wastes whatever is never written, typically well over half, and fragments as requests finish. Paging with page size $p$ wastes at most $p - 1$ tokens per request and never fragments: a request's cache is a list of page numbers, and the attention kernel gathers through that list. In SGLang, `TokenToKVPool` is the page pool (page size 1 by default), `req_to_token` holds the page tables, and `retract_decode` is the eviction when nothing frees up. Sharing pages across requests is the next chapter.
