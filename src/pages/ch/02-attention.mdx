<summary>The math, once</summary>

For one head, with $Q = XW_Q$, $K = XW_K$, $V = XW_V$:

$$
\mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^{\top}}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V
$$

Row $i$ of the result only uses row $i$ of $Q$, but every row $\le i$ of $K$ and $V$. That asymmetry is the whole next chapter.
