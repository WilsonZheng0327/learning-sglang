<summary>The math, once</summary>

For one head, with $Q = XW_Q$, $K = XW_K$, $V = XW_V$:

$$
\mathrm{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^{\top}}{\sqrt{d_k}} + M\right)V
$$

$M$ is the causal mask from step 7: $M_{ij} = -\infty$ for $j > i$ and $0$ otherwise, so softmax gives a later key zero weight. Row $i$ of the result only uses row $i$ of $Q$, but every row $\le i$ of $K$ and $V$. That asymmetry is the whole next chapter.
